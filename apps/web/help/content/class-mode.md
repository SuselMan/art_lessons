With a group, drawing on one shared sheet only goes so far. **Assignments** give every student a blank board of their own for the same task, while you watch all of them at once and step into any one to correct it.

Everything here lives in the **Class** tab of the right-hand panel. There's nothing to switch on.

## Hand out an assignment

1. In the Class tab, under **Where the class is**, press **New assignment**.
2. Name it (it defaults to *Assignment 1*, *Assignment 2*…) and press **Hand out**.

Every student in the lesson gets a blank board of their own, named after them, and is taken to it. A student who joins later gets theirs when you next send the class to that assignment.

**Where the class is** lists **With me** (your board) and every assignment you've handed out, with a mark on the one the class is on now. Next to the others:

- **Everyone to me** — brings the class back to your board.
- **Send everyone here** — sends the class to that assignment again.

Students can keep working on their own board after you call them back.

## Watch everyone at once

**All works** on an assignment opens a full-screen grid of every student's board:

- a live thumbnail of each work,
- the student's name and whether they're **In the lesson** or **Not in the lesson**,
- a raised hand, if there is one — those come first,
- how fresh it is: *updated just now*, *updated 3 min ago*, or *Nothing yet*.

Thumbnails refresh a few seconds after the student's pen comes to rest, so they don't flicker while someone is mid-stroke.

## Step into one student's work

Open a tile and you're on that student's board. A bar at the top gives you **Class** (back to the grid), **Previous student** / **Next student**, and **Show everyone**. The student sees *The teacher is looking at your work*.

You arrive holding the annotation pen, so what you draw is a remark **over** their work, not in it. Switch on **Edit the work** when you do want to draw into the student's own layers. Your previous tool comes back when you leave.

## Show one student's work to everyone

**Show everyone** on a tile (or in the bar) takes the class to that work, with a chip that reads *The teacher is showing …'s work*. **Stop showing** ends it.

## Raised hands

Students have **Raise hand** in the header for the whole lesson, not only during assignments. You see the count on the Class tab; tap a hand to lower it.

## Can students see each other's work?

By default, no — only you open the grid. Turn on **Students see each other's work** (**Menu → Settings → Tools**, or when creating the project) and students can open the grid too. A classmate's board is read-only for them: *…'s work — you can look, not draw*.

> Every personal board starts blank. Assignments can't be renamed or deleted yet, and there's no separate "teacher's correction" layer — to keep your marks apart from the student's, draw them as remarks (the default) or add a layer of your own.

On a phone, the class grid isn't available — see [Leave notes on a drawing](/help/notes/).
