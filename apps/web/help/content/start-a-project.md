A lesson in Grafetto happens in a **project**: one shared sheet of paper that you and your students draw on at the same time. You create it, send the link, and everyone who opens the link is on the same sheet.

You don't need an account to start. Grafetto remembers you in this browser; an account only matters once you want your projects listed in one place (see [Keep your projects organised](/help/my-projects/)).

## Create the project

Open Grafetto and you land on **New project**. It has three tabs.

### Project

- **Project name (optional)** — up to 50 characters. Left empty, it's called *Untitled*. You can rename it later by clicking the name in the editor's header.
- **Paper texture** — **Coarse**, **Medium**, **Fine** or **Flat**. This is the tooth every tool reacts to: graphite and charcoal catch on a coarse sheet and glide over a fine one. It is **fixed once the project is created**, so pick it for the medium you'll teach.
- **Paper color** — the small swatch next to the texture heading. **Use default** puts it back.
- **Canvas size** — **A4**, **A3** (the default), **A2**, **Square**, **16:9**, or **Custom** (any width and height from 100 to 4096 px). Click a selected A-size again, or its rotate badge, to switch between portrait and landscape.

### Access

Who can get in. The same settings are available later inside the project — see [Who can join](#who-can-join) below.

### Tools

Which tools everyone in the project gets — you included. Leave them all on for a general lesson, or narrow the set down: a lesson on tonal drawing with **pencil only** is a single tick box away. At least one drawing material has to stay on. You can change this later under **Menu → Settings → Tools**.

This tab also holds **Students see each other's work**, which matters for [class mode](/help/class-mode/).

Press **Create project** and you're in. You are the project's **owner**.

## Send the link

Inside the project, open the menu (**≡**) in the header and choose **Share**. On a computer the link is copied — you'll see **Link copied** — and you paste it into your chat or email. On a tablet, Share opens the system share sheet instead.

You can also share from [My Projects](/help/my-projects/): the **⋮** menu on the project's card has the same **Share**.

## What your student sees

The student opens the link in a browser and sees **Join "your project name"** with a single field, **Your name**. They type it and press **Join project**. That's all — no account, no app to install.

If the project has a password, a password field appears after the first try, with *This project is password-protected — enter it to join.*

> Your student's name is what appears next to their cursor and in your **Class** tab, so it's worth asking them to use their real one.

## Who can join

As the owner you manage access under **Menu → Settings → Access** (or **⋮ → Access** on the project's card in My Projects).

- **Who can join**
  - **Anyone with the link** — whoever has the link gets in.
  - **Only people you invite** — add email addresses under **Invited**. An invitation takes effect when that person signs in with exactly that address. Anyone else who opens the link can ask to join and waits until you press **Let in** or **Not now** (the requests also appear at the top of the **Class** tab).
- **Password** — optional, and it works in both modes. You, the owner, are never asked for it.
- **Been in this project** — everyone who has joined. **Revoke access** removes a person: they can no longer draw and can't come back until you **Restore access**.

Switching to *Only people you invite* doesn't throw out people who are already in; it only stops new ones.
