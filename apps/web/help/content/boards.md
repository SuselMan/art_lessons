A project can hold several **boards**: separate sheets inside the same lesson. Use one per exercise — a warm-up, the main study, a detail — instead of crowding everything onto a single sheet.

Boards share the project's paper, size, access, tools and frozen/closed state. Each board has its own drawing and its own layers.

## Open the boards

Press **Boards** in the header (on a narrow screen it's in the **≡** menu). A strip opens with every board as a thumbnail, its name, and how many people are on it. The board the teacher is on carries a small school icon.

As the owner you always see the Boards button. Students see it once there are at least two boards.

## Add, rename, reorder, delete

- **New board** adds a board after the others and takes you to it. It's named after the project with a number: *Still life 2*, *Still life 3*…
- The **⋮** on a board: **Rename**, **Move left** / **Move right**, **Delete board**.
- Deleting asks first — everything drawn on that board goes with it, for everyone, and it can't be undone.

The first board is the project itself: it can't be deleted or moved.

## Take the class with you

When you switch boards, every student who is **following** you goes too. Following is on for everyone by default, so for most lessons you simply change boards and the class comes along.

A student can wander off to another board on their own. They then stop following, and see a chip — **Teacher is on "…"** — that brings them back to your board and turns following on again.

Anyone who opens the link or reloads the page lands on the board you are on.

## Copying boards

- **Make a copy** in [My Projects](/help/my-projects/) copies the whole project, all boards included.
- **Take a copy** inside a closed project copies only the board on screen — see [Give homework and review it](/help/homework/).

> Thumbnails of boards other people have drawn on refresh when you reload the page.
