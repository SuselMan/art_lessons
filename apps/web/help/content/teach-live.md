Once your student has joined, you're both on the same sheet. Everything drawn by anyone appears for everyone as it's being drawn — not when the stroke ends.

Keep your usual video call running alongside: Grafetto is the paper, not the camera.

## See where everyone is

Other people's cursors show on the sheet as a dot with their name. The tag stays the same size and upright however you zoom or turn your own view, and fades out when that person's pen has been still for a while.

## Your view is yours

Zoom, pan and rotation are **not shared**. Turn the sheet the way you'd turn paper on a desk, zoom right into a detail — your student's screen doesn't move.

- **Rotation** — the angle in the header. Drag it up or down to turn, click it for a quarter turn. The lock beside it (**Lock canvas rotation**) keeps two-finger gestures from turning the sheet by accident.
- **Zoom** — the percentage in the header. Drag to adjust, click to go back to 100%. **Fit canvas** shows the whole sheet.
- On a keyboard: <kbd>R</kbd> resets rotation, <kbd>0</kbd> goes to 100%. All the keys are in [Keyboard shortcuts and gestures](/help/shortcuts/).

## Correct on a layer of your own

The cleanest way to correct a drawing is on its own layer, so the student's work stays untouched underneath:

1. Open the **Layers** panel and press **Add layer**.
2. Draw your correction on it — perspective lines, a corrected contour, a tone scale.
3. Hide it with the eye button when you want the student to try again, and show it to compare.

Hiding a layer hides it **for everyone**. If you only want to look at one layer yourself, use **Show only this layer** instead — that one is private to your screen.

Quick remarks that shouldn't touch the drawing at all go better as [notes](/help/notes/).

## Bring in a reference

**Import reference image** in the Layers panel puts a photo or a study on a new layer above the current one. Any image your browser can open works, up to 8 MB. Lower the layer's **Opacity** to draw over it, or lock it so nobody moves it by accident.

## Keep the class in hand

The **Class** tab in the right-hand panel lists everyone in the project: who is drawing, who is just watching, and whose hand is up.

- **Freeze project** (the snowflake in the Class tab) pauses drawing for everyone while you explain. Press it again to let everyone draw.
- To pause one person, use the **⋮** on their row → **Freeze drawing**.
- You, the owner, are never frozen.

Freezing lasts for the lesson; it isn't saved with the project.
