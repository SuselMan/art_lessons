## Two negative results

Mask-normalized convolution is designed to handle uncertain/missing samples, not to guarantee pigment mass or wet connectivity. Our tiny counterexamples: uniform radius1filter of mass[1,0,0] on3wet cells gives total5/6; radius2 across wet/dry/wet gives right-side .5 although disconnected. Row normalization alone is not a conservative no-crossing contract. These are OUR counterexamples, not criticism of its actual interpolation purpose. Primary context: [Knutsson/Westin publication list,1993](https://staff.gitlab-pages.liu.se/publications/hankn25/) and [LiU normalized convolution lecture](https://www.bb.isy.liu.se/education/undergraduate/tsbb06/lectures/2C-SubspacesNC.pdf). [Perona–Malik1990](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~malik/papers/MP-aniso.pdf) motivates spatial conductivity, but this nonlocal kernel is NOT their local nonlinear PDE solver.

## Dense conservative alternative

Fresh fine mobile average is packed16 at256². Every cell exchanges with ALL integer radii1..24 or1..32 along8rays, diagonals limited to the SAME physical circular radius (floor(R/√2));160/216donors. Weights r·|direction|²·exp(−distance²/2σ²) approximate polar-area quadrature, compensate diagonal sample spacing, sum to .75. This gives equal second moments in x/y; it is not full rotation invariance (four orientations remain). No dyadic or DFS endpoint copies. Shader uses precompiled Gaussian recurrence constants, not per-fragment exp.

Both endpoint conductivity min(Wi,Wj) and whole-segment positive-water certificate are symmetric. Missing/blocked traffic stays at source instead of renormalizing accepted neighbours. This is a conservative nonlocal diffusion kernel, not directional fluid advection. CPU raw4×4 min-water creates coarse domain; O(8Ncoarse) run-length maps certify every cardinal/diagonal path, diagonals include both corner cells (supercover). Thus raw dry stripes cannot be shortcut. No pigment readback or input model mutation.
